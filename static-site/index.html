— FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an Automatic Rescue Device (ARD)?

An ARD is an emergency safety system that activates during power failures, automatically moving the elevator to the nearest floor and opening its doors so passengers can exit safely.

Do you provide installation across Delhi NCR?

Yes. Our trained engineers handle installation, commissioning and after-sales support across all of Delhi NCR and most major Indian cities.

Which elevators are compatible with your Lift UPS?

Our UPS systems are compatible with most single-phase and three-phase elevators from leading OEMs. Our team will confirm compatibility during the quotation stage.

Do you offer dealer or distributor partnerships?

Yes. We welcome elevator service companies and industry professionals to join our dealer network. Reach out via the contact form or WhatsApp to discuss terms.

What warranty do your products come with?

All our ARD and Lift UPS products come with a standard one-year warranty. Extended service contracts are available on request.
