# Tycoon Elevators - Product Requirements Document

## Project Overview
**Business Name:** Tycoon Elevators  
**Location:** Delhi NCR, India  
**Project Type:** Premium Elevator Company Website  
**Start Date:** December 2025

## Original Problem Statement
Create a modern, premium and service-focused website for an elevator company that strongly highlights services and convinces users to contact the company. The website should be visually rich, professional, with white/light background and gold accents using Poppins typography.

## User Personas
1. **Residential Building Owners** - Looking for reliable passenger lifts for apartments and housing complexes
2. **Commercial Property Managers** - Need high-speed elevators for corporate towers and office buildings
3. **Healthcare Administrators** - Require specialized medical elevator systems for hospitals and clinics
4. **Industrial Facility Managers** - Need heavy-duty goods lifts for warehouses and factories
5. **Individual Homeowners** - Seeking compact and elegant home elevator solutions
